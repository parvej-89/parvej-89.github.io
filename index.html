Game Booster

Check your connection, get ready, and play. Made for eFootball, Free Fire, PUBG Mobile and Call of Duty.

What it does

  • Measures your ping, jitter and packet loss before you play.
  • Sets a fast DNS and silences notifications.
  • Uses a faster route only when it measures as faster.
  • Tells you when to play and when to wait.
  • Sends a notification when your connection becomes good or weak.

Honest notes

Ping shown is measured to nearby server regions, not inside the game. There is no fake RAM or FPS boosting.
