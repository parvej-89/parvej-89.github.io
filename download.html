How to install

The app is not on the Play Store, so Android asks a few questions. This is normal.

  1. Tap DownloadYour browser saves a file called GameBooster.apk.
  2. Open the fileTap the download notification, or open it from your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow installs from this sourceIf Android says it is blocked, tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source for your browser or files app. Then go back and tap Install.
  4. If Play Protect warns youIt says the developer is unknown because the app is outside the Play Store. Choose Install anyway.

Get started in 2 minutes

  1. Create your accountUse your email and a password, or tap Continue with Google. For email sign-up, open the link we send you to verify it, then come back and log in.
  2. Pick your gameOn the Home tab choose eFootball, Free Fire, PUBG Mobile or Call of Duty. The game must already be installed.
  3. Tap GET READYThe app turns on Do Not Disturb, closes background apps, picks the fastest DNS and checks your connection. If your normal ping is under 90 ms it opens the game right away. If not, it first tests the faster route, which takes about 10 to 25 seconds, then opens the game on whichever is better. A small bar appears on top of the game.
  4. Allow the permissions, onceDisplay over other apps (for the bar), notifications, and Do Not Disturb access. If you use the faster route, Android also shows a VPN request. Tap OK.
  5. Play, then look at your sessionWhen you finish, open the Home tab to see the graph of your last game.

DIRECTPing of your normal connection, in milliseconds. Lower is better.
ROUTEPing when the faster route is on. It shows off when you are on your normal line, and lost if the route stops answering.
STABLE %How steady your line is. 90% or more is stable, 75% to 90% is shaky, lower is unstable.
LOSSShare of checks that got no answer. A check counts as lost only after a second try also fails. Anything above 0% can feel like freezing.
SPKSpikes: sudden jumps in ping. A few are normal. Many in a row means a bad line. With the route on it shows both, for example D9 R7 for direct and route.
JITJitter: how much the ping changes from one check to the next. Small is smooth. With the route on it shows direct and route, like 26/0.
Wi-FiWi-Fi signal in dBm. Closer to zero is stronger: -55 or better is excellent, -67 good, -75 weak. It shows Mobile data when you are not on Wi-Fi.
33°C 38%Phone temperature and battery, then how long the session has run. The bar warns when the phone is hot.
FPSHow many frames per second the screen is updating while you play. In a match it matches the game. On still menus it reads lower because the screen is not changing. Needs a one-time setup, see Advanced below.
RAMFree memory. When it runs low the bar says LOW MEMORY. The bar only warns you. It never closes your game.
USEDData the phone has used since the session started. It sits under the minus and X buttons.

The colors

Green: 80 ms or less. Great.

Yellow: 81 to 130 ms. Playable, a little slow.

Red: over 130 ms, or a lost reply. You will feel it.

The bar edge turns red for a moment when a spike happens.

Faster route

Found on the Home tab, under the game you picked. It is optional.

The faster route sends your game's traffic through a private Cloudflare WARP tunnel built into the app. Sometimes that path is quicker or steadier. Sometimes it is not, so the app tests it first and shows you the result instead of promising one.

How the app decides

  • Normal ping under 90 ms: the game opens on your normal line. There is nothing to improve, so the route is not tested.
  • Normal ping 90 ms or more: before the game opens, the app measures your normal line, turns the route on and measures it again. It keeps the route only if it is at least 10 ms faster, loses no more packets and is not shakier. Otherwise it switches the route off and opens the game on your normal line.
  • During the match: the app never turns the route on, so it cannot interrupt a match to start it.
  • Going back to normal: if the route stops answering for about 6 seconds, or stays clearly slower than your normal line (40 ms or more for 20 seconds), the app switches back. Any change of connection can make a game pause or reconnect for a moment, so the app only does this when staying would be worse.

Tips for a smoother game

  • Always start from GET READY. It closes background apps, picks the fastest DNS and decides whether the route is worth using before the game opens.
  • Use 5 GHz Wi-Fi close to the router. A Wi-Fi signal weaker than -75 dBm causes lag that no booster can fix.
  • Pause downloads and updates. If the bar says BUSY LINE, something else is using your internet.
  • Do not judge by one number. One spike is normal. Look at STABLE % and the session graph.
  • Keep the phone cool. A hot phone slows the game. Take the case off and do not charge while you play.
  • Watch for LOW MEMORY. The bar only warns you, so you decide what to close.
  • Switch off battery saver. It can slow the game and the bar.
  • Try both Wi-Fi and mobile data with Network doctor. The better one changes by time of day.
  • Check the best hours. Evenings are often busy. The app tracks when your line is best.
  • Use the test, not a feeling. Run Test with faster route once. Keep it on only if the graph shows it helped.
  • Use your phone's game mode. Many phones have a built-in game mode with extra touch and network options. Add your game there too.

Advanced: turn on FPS (optional)

How to enable the FPS counter

Android keeps frame data private. You can allow it once with a single command. It does not need root and works only on some phones, so it is optional.

  1. Settings, About phone, tap Build number 7 times to turn on Developer options.
  2. In Developer options turn on Wireless debugging (and on some Xiaomi phones also USB debugging (Security settings)).
  3. In Termux (or any adb tool) pair and connect with the codes the phone shows, then run:

adb shell pm grant com.gamebooster.game_booster android.permission.DUMP

Restart the app and start your game from Game Booster. If FPS cannot be read, the bar tells you why.

Questions

Will it lower my ping?

It shows your real ping, cleans up your phone before you play, and can try a faster route. Results depend on your phone, your internet provider and the game server. If your normal ping is already good, the app leaves it alone. Use Test with faster route to see the truth for your connection. No app can promise lower ping everywhere.

Can the route make the game disconnect?

Any change of connection can make a game pause or reconnect for a moment. That is why the app tests the route before the game opens and never turns it on during a match. It switches back to your normal line only if the route stops answering or is clearly worse.

What does GET READY close?

It closes background apps that are not part of the system and are not your games. Chat or music apps may restart or need to be opened again. Your game and Game Booster are never closed.

Why does the FPS number look low on menus?

The FPS value is how often the screen updates. In a match that follows your game. On still menus the screen is not changing, so the number is lower. That is normal.

Why does Android warn me when I install it?

The app is not on the Play Store, so Play Protect shows an unknown developer warning for every app like this. Choose Install anyway.

Does it change or hack my game?

No. It never touches the game files. It measures your connection, shows a bar on top of the screen and can use a VPN-style tunnel. Games and publishers set their own rules for third-party tools, so use it at your own choice.

Why does it ask for VPN permission?

Only the faster route needs it. Android shows that request for any app that creates a tunnel. If you never use the route, you can say no.

Why are there ads?
