Game Booster

Checks your connection before you play eFootball, Free Fire, PUBG Mobile and Call of Duty, and shows your ping live while you play.

Download for Android
What you get
  • GET READY: fast DNS, Do Not Disturb, connection check
  • A small floating ping bar over your game
  • Optional faster route, graphs of your last games
How to install
  1. Tap Download and open the file.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or files app.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, choose Install anyway. The app is not on the Play Store.
Android only. Free to use, with ads. Premium removes them.
